# English Lesson Plan: Storytelling Basics

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
- Identify the main parts of a story (Beginning, Middle, End)
- Understand "Characters" and "Setting"
- Create a simple story using a prompt
- Use descriptive words (adjectives)

---

## Part 1: Parts of a Story (Easy)

### The 3 Big Parts
Every good story has three parts, like a sandwich!
1. **Beginning (The Top Bun):** Introduces the *Characters* and the *Setting*.
2. **Middle (The Tasty Filling):** Where the problem or action happens.
3. **End (The Bottom Bun):** How things solve or finish.

### Key Vocabulary
- **Character:** A person, animal, or creature in the story.
- **Setting:** Where and when the story takes place.
- **Plot:** What happens in the story.

### Example
*The Three Little Pigs*
- **Beginning:** Three pigs build houses.
- **Middle:** A wolf tries to blow them down.
- **End:** The wolf cannot blow down the brick house, and the pigs are safe.

### Questions
1. Who is the "bad guy" in the Three Little Pigs? (The Wolf)
2. What is the setting of the third pig's house? (A brick house)

---

## Part 2: Describing Things (Medium)

### Adjectives
Adjectives are **describing words**. They make stories colorful!
- Instead of "The dog barked," say "The **big, fluffy** dog barked."
- Instead of "He ran," say "He ran **quickly**."

### Practice
Add a describing word to these sentences:
1. The ________ cat slept on the rug.
2. The ________ sun shone in the sky.
3. She ate a ________ apple.

**Possible Answers:**
1. sleepy / black / fat
2. bright / yellow / hot
3. red / juicy / crunchy

## Part 3: Story Starters (Hard)

### Let's Write a Story!
Use your imagination to finish these ideas.

**Prompt 1:**
One day, I found a magic key in my backyard. It was glowing blue. I looked for what it opened and found...

**Prompt 2:**
A friendly dragon landed on the school playground. He looked sad because he lost his...

### Discussion Questions
- If you found a magic key, what would you want it to open?
- How would you help a sad dragon?

---

## Part 4: Creative Challenge

**Write a Short Story (3-5 sentences)**
Include:
1. A Character (Who?)
2. A Setting (Where?)
3. A Problem (What goes wrong?)
4. A Solution (How is it fixed?)

**Example:**
*Timmy the Turtle (Character) was at the beach (Setting). He flipped over on his back and couldn't get up (Problem). A friendly crab pushed him back over, and Timmy swam away happy (Solution).*

---

## Tips for Teachers
- **Read Aloud:** Read stories with clear structures.
- **Act it Out:** Have students act out beginnings, middles, and ends.
- **Draw:** Ask students to draw their characters before writing about them.
