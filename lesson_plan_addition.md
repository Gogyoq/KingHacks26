# Addition Lesson Plan for Elementary Students

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
- Understand addition as combining groups
- Solve basic addition problems (sums up to 20)
- Apply addition to real-world scenarios
- Recognize addition vocabulary: plus, add, sum, total, altogether

---

## Part 1: Introduction to Addition (Easy)

### What is Addition?
Addition means **putting together** or combining two or more groups to find out how many there are in total.

**Key vocabulary:**
- **Plus (+)**: The addition symbol
- **Sum**: The answer to an addition problem
- **Equals (=)**: Is the same as

### Basic Examples
1. You have 2 red apples and 1 green apple. How many apples total?
   - 2 + 1 = 3 apples

2. One hand has 5 fingers. The other hand has 5 fingers.
   - 5 + 5 = 10 fingers

3. 3 birds are on the ground. 2 more land.
   - 3 + 2 = 5 birds

### Practice Problems (Easy)
1. 2 + 2 = ?
2. 3 + 1 = ?
3. 4 + 1 = ?
4. 5 + 2 = ?
5. 1 + 6 = ?

**Real-world scenario:**
You have 3 pencils. Your friend gives you 2 more. How many pencils do you have now?

## Part 2: Addition Strategies (Medium)

### Strategy 1: Counting On
Start with the bigger number and count up.

**Example:** 5 + 3 = ?
- Start at 5.
- Count up 3: "6, 7, 8"
- Answer: 8

### Strategy 2: Doubles
Memorizing doubles helps you add fast!
- 1 + 1 = 2
- 2 + 2 = 4
- 3 + 3 = 6
- 4 + 4 = 8
- 5 + 5 = 10

**Near Doubles:**
If you know 4 + 4 = 8, then 4 + 5 is just one more (9).

### Practice Problems (Medium)
1. 6 + 4 = ?
2. 5 + 5 = ?
3. 7 + 3 = ?
4. 8 + 2 = ?
5. 6 + 6 = ?

**Real-world scenario:**
You found 5 shells on the beach. Later, you found 4 more. How many shells did you find in total?

---

## Part 3: Two-Digit Addition (Hard)

### Adding without Regrouping
Add the ones, then add the tens.

**Example:** 12 + 15 = ?
- Ones: 2 + 5 = 7
- Tens: 10 + 10 = 20
- Answer: 27

### Make a Ten
When adding numbers like 8 + 5, think:
- 8 needs 2 more to make 10.
- Take 2 from 5 (leaving 3).
- 10 + 3 = 13.

### Practice Problems (Hard)
1. 10 + 10 = ?
2. 12 + 8 = ?
3. 15 + 4 = ?
4. 11 + 9 = ?
5. 9 + 6 = ?

**Real-world scenario:**
A class has 12 boys and 13 girls. How many students are in the class?

---

## Part 4: Challenge Problems

1. 20 + 20 = ?
2. 15 + 15 = ?

4. 50 + 50 = ?

**Super Challenge:**
If you save 10 dollars every week for 4 weeks, how much money do you have?
- 10 + 10 + 10 + 10 = 40 dollars
